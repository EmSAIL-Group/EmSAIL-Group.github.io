ABOUT EMSAIL

Building Intelligence for the Physical World

EmSAIL (Embodied Spatial AI Lab) is a research laboratory at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), dedicated to advancing spatial intelligence and embodied AI.

Our long-term vision is to build robots that continuously perceive their surroundings, understand tasks, develop persistent spatial memories, predict future changes, and act autonomously in complex real-world environments.

We bridge intelligent algorithms and physical robotic systems, pursuing an integrated approach from robot hardware and spatial perception to memory, reasoning, and action.

Perceive Understand the physical world
Remember & Reason Build memory and predict the future
Act Learn and execute real-world skills

Research

Research Directions

Spatial Intelligence

Advancing 3D/4D spatial perception, dynamic scene understanding, and persistent spatial memory for robots operating in complex, evolving environments.

Foundation Models

Developing embodied world models, vision-language-action models, and spatial reasoning to connect multimodal understanding, future prediction, and intelligent decision-making.

Robot Learning & Systems

Integrating robot policy learning with in-house hardware development, including manipulation, navigation, tactile sensing, and robotic platforms for real-world embodied intelligence.

Autonomous Quadruped Navigation

Developing an autonomous LRN (localization, reconstruction, and navigation) system for the AS2 quadruped robot, integrating SLAM, real-time localization, mapping, path planning, and obstacle avoidance for operation in complex real-world environments.
